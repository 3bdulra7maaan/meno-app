# App name

Meno – Ask someone who's tried

# Short description

Ask the Sudanese community and share useful everyday experiences.

# Full description

Meno helps the Sudanese community exchange practical questions and answers based on real experiences.

Browse approved questions without signing in. Search everyday topics, submit a question for moderation, and choose whether to hide your display name. Share a useful answer or mark an answer as helpful.

Topics include travel, transfers, housing, education, services and more. Meno has a lightweight Arabic-first, right-to-left interface.

Community responses reflect individual experiences. They are not a substitute for official information or professional medical or legal advice. Never include sensitive personal information in public posts.
